# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We judge contributions — code, docs, issues, reviews — on their content. Behaviors that make
this a professional, focused community: using welcoming and inclusive language, being respectful
of differing viewpoints and experience, accepting constructive criticism, showing empathy,
and giving credit where due. Unacceptable: harassment, trolling, insulting/derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, or any conduct a
reasonable professional would consider inappropriate in a workplace.

## Scope

Project spaces (repo, issues, PRs, chats) and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers (contact in SECURITY.md; conduct matters may use the same
private channel). Maintainers will acknowledge within 72h. Consequences for unacceptable
behavior range from private warning → temporary ban from project spaces → permanent ban,
proportionate to severity and recurrence. All moderation decisions are logged privately;
appeals go to maintainers not involved in the original decision.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant; this document is the operative version.
